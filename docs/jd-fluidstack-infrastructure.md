## Role summary

A software engineering role embedded in infrastructure delivery, connecting
construction plans, procurement, field progress, and commissioning in shared
data and workflows.

## Main areas of work

* Model project dependencies so design and schedule changes affect material
  demand and procurement timing.
* Connect field observations and inspections to the relevant design versions.
* Implement change-control workflows spanning commercial approvals and
  downstream project records.
* Integrate building models, asset records, and commissioning evidence.

## Experience emphasized

Production software delivery in Go, Python, or TypeScript; independent
problem-solving; and practical collaboration with construction and field
specialists. Relevant domain experience includes scheduling, building models,
commissioning systems, document control, and graph-based data.
