## Role summary

A software engineering role focused on the operation and maintenance of
physical compute infrastructure, with tools that guide technicians and
connect incidents, equipment, repairs, and staffing.

## Main areas of work

* Relate alerts and incident investigation to equipment records and on-call
  response.
* Track repair, spare-part replacement, and vendor returns.
* Coordinate task dependencies, shift assignments, and maintenance work.
* Unify facility and IT asset management, replacing fragmented operational
  tooling with guided procedures and recorded checks.

## Experience emphasized

Production software delivery in Go, Python, or TypeScript; direct exposure
to on-call work or operational teams; and interfaces suited to technicians.
Experience with maintenance, data-center, warehouse, or physical-fleet
management software is particularly relevant.
