## Role summary

An embedded software engineering role serving finance, legal, and people
operations. The emphasis is on connecting business records and approvals
to the infrastructure delivery process.

## Main areas of work

* Integrate recruiting, interview capacity, and onboarding workflows.
* Derive spending forecasts and payment timing from commercial commitments
  and construction progress.
* Connect capital approvals and funding releases to supporting evidence.
* Extract actionable contract information and support accounting and
  reconciliation processes.

## Experience emphasized

Production software delivery in Go, Python, or TypeScript; independent
execution; and adoption by non-engineering specialists under real business
deadlines. Relevant backgrounds include enterprise finance systems, treasury,
contract management, recruiting integrations, and document extraction.
