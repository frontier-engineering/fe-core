## Role summary

A software engineering role working alongside procurement, factory, quality,
and warehouse teams to connect hardware requirements with purchasing,
delivery, inspection, and payment.

## Main areas of work

* Link bills of materials, pricing, lead times, and purchase orders.
* Detect procurement delays and inconsistencies using vendor information
  and project dependencies.
* Reconcile received goods, serialized inventory, and financial records.
* Maintain manufacturing design lineage and connect quality checks to
  nonconformance handling and supplier assessment.

## Experience emphasized

Production software delivery in Go, Python, or TypeScript; extensible data
models; and practical adoption by operational teams. Relevant experience
includes manufacturing and logistics systems, component traceability,
engineering change control, supplier quality, and vendor-document extraction.
